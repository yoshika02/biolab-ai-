1. Platform Overview
BioLab AI is a unified, AI-powered laboratory intelligence platform built specifically for biotechnology research institutions. It replaces scattered tools — protocol binders, Excel inventory sheets, paper lab notebooks, and manual literature searches — with a single intelligent system that understands the domain, learns from usage, and actively assists researchers in their daily work.

1.1 Problem Statement
• Researchers waste 2–4 hours per day searching through protocol PDFs, papers, and lab binders
• Inventory is tracked in disconnected Excel sheets with no expiry alerts or reorder predictions
• Experiment logs are handwritten or in individual files — no anomaly detection, no pattern spotting
• Chemical safety information requires navigating to multiple external websites per query
• No unified view of lab activity for lab heads and department heads

1.2 Solution
BioLab AI provides 7 interconnected AI modules, all sharing a single database and authentication system, accessible from a unified dashboard. Researchers interact with natural language; the platform responds with cited, accurate, domain-aware answers.

Features
• FASTA input: paste raw sequence or FASTA-formatted input. Auto-strip header line if present.
• Biopython computation (Python service): sliding window over sequence to find candidate 18–25 bp primers. Compute Tm (nearest-neighbor method), GC%, hairpin and dimer prediction.
• Quality score: weighted composite — Tm difference (penalise if >2°C apart), GC% (penalise if outside 40–60%), secondary structure score, amplicon size fit.
• BLAST off-target check: submit primer pair to NCBI BLAST API (blastn) against genome of interest. Parse hit count into off_target_hits column.
• Claude AI explanation: for top 3 pairs, prompt Claude to explain in plain English why this primer pair scores well or has caveats.
• Save selected pair: link to experiment, add to primer_designs. Linked primers appear in Experiment detail > Reagents tab.
